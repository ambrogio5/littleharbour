Little Harbour privacy policy
Effective 5 November 2026

Your town belongs to you
Little Harbour works offline. The app does not require an account and does not send your town, device identifiers or gameplay activity to us. It includes no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party online services.

What stays on your device
Your towns, construction and production progress, town evolution, partner and civic progress, city voyages and recovery backups are saved in the app’s private storage. Separate coast saves and any town names you choose are stored there too. Presentation preferences, such as building labels, map position and zoom, and haptic feedback, are saved on your device. These files may be included in your device’s system backups according to your Apple backup settings. Little Harbour does not provide its own cloud sync.

Imports and exports
Importing a save reads only the file you select. Before a town is replaced, the app keeps an exact recovery copy on the device. When you export or share a save, you choose the destination. The selected app or service’s own privacy policy applies after you share it. Save files contain game progress and the town name you choose; no personal information is required. A native export backs up the selected town, not the entire coast library. Inspect a save before sharing it if you have put personal information in a town name.

Control and deletion
You can export your town, restore an earlier backup, or start a new town from Settings & saves. Starting a new town retains the previous town as a recovery backup. Deleting Little Harbour from the device removes its local app data; offloading the app retains that data. Exported copies and system backups must be managed separately in their respective apps or services.

Support
The Get help link opens the project’s public GitHub issue tracker. Opening that website is optional and subject to GitHub’s privacy policy. Anything you choose to post in a public issue can be read by others. Do not include personal details in a bug report; a screenshot and the app version are usually sufficient. If you choose to attach a save, inspect it first.

Changes
This policy describes the native 1.0 release. If future versions add online features, this policy and the app’s disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
